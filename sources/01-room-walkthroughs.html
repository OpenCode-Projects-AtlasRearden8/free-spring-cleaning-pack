Room-by-Room Walk-Throughs

Pick a room, start at the door, and work one gentle lap around it.

Room:

Date started:   Finished:

Every room, first lap

  • Trash to the bin — do this before anything elseClearing obvious trash first makes every later decision easier.
  • Dishes, cups, and food out of the roomThey always find their way to odd rooms.
  • Get-from-here-to-home pile: mail, chargers, toysReturn stray items to the room they belong in.
  • Dust surfaces top to bottom (high shelves → low)Gravity is on your side if you start high.
  • Skirting boards, door frames, light switchesEveryone forgets these; they show up in the light.
  • Floors: sweep/vacuum, then a quick mop if it wants one
  • One “losing spots” check: under things, behind thingsA peek, not a project. Note anything bigger for the Deep-Clean sheet.

Bedroom extras

  • Under-bed sweep (rescue the socks)
  • Nightstand surfaces
  • Fluff & flip bedding; wash pillow covers
  • Closet: one easy shelf, one easy rod
  • Fresh-air five: open the window

Living room extras

  • Cushion fluff & couch crevice check
  • Fix-a-book shelf tidy
  • Remote/coaster/tray gathering
  • Throw blankets washed or shaken out
  • Electronics cords wrangled once more

Kitchen extras

  • Fridge: toss the experiments
  • Pantry front row only (no full audit today)
  • Counter pile returned — the “counter-to-nowhere” pile
  • Sink answered: dishes done or rinsed
  • One drawer chosen and emptied fully

Bathroom extras

  • Expired toss-ups: makeup, sunscreen, meds*
  • Towels: laundry pile decided
  • Cabinet overflow pass (things living on counters)
  • Mirror & tap shine
  • Wash cloth on the baseboards

Entry / hallway extras

  • Coat hook unpile (keep the three most-loved)
  • Shoe zone: one full round-trip
  • Misc-drawer drop zone confirmed

Office / desk (folded into the above)

  • Flat-surface clear: desk completely visible
  • Folder or accessory pouch emptied fully
  • Old papers filed, shredded, or recycled

Keep this sheet in the room while you work. Anything on one of these lists can be logged as “laundry done” or “head out the door” straight onto the Freecycle & Donate Drop Log. *Medication & medical supplies: you know your processes, but never flush them; ask your pharmacist what they will accept.
