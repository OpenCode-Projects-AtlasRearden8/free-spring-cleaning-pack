One-Hour Zone Timers

One room zone, five small stretches, one honest break. Go.

Today's zone:

Start time:   Total zones done this week:

0:00 – 0:10

Stretch 1 • 10 min — Trash & speed return

Bin empty, dishes out, obvious junk gathered. Grab stray cups and trash on your very first walk in. Nothing detailed, nothing precious — just clear and quick.

0:10 – 0:25

Stretch 2 • 15 min — Home base reset

Return every stray item to where it belongs. Don't fix barely-organized corners here — just get things home. If it has no home, it goes in a “decide later” box so it isn't staring at you for the rest of the hour.

0:25 – 0:40

Stretch 3 • 15 min — Surfaces & shine

Dust and wipe from top down: shelves, counters, tabletops, switch plates. Finish with the floor. A clean surface is what makes a room feel reset, even if one corner is still being decided on.

0:40 – 0:45

Stretch 4 • 5 min — Sit-down break. Water. Phone down.

This is not skippable. Sit in the cleaned zone and enjoy it for five full minutes. The stretch that you skip is the one that makes the last stretch miserable.

0:45 – 1:00

Stretch 5 • 15 min — Finish the floor

Vacuum or mop, then leave the room. Tour it once on the way out with the door open. Jot down what worked, what got skipped, and what goes on the Drop Log.
