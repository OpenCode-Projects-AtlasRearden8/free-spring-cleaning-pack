Getting Started

A friendly, no-pressure way to make your home feel lighter again.

Hello, friend.

Welcome in. If you picked this pack up, something in your home is probably bothering you — a pile here, a drawer there, a whole room you keep meaning to get to. That's okay. Homes gather stuff the way kitchens gather crumbs. This pack isn't about a perfect, magazine-ready house. It's about walking through your space, opening a few windows of time, and putting things back where they belong.

How to start (gently)

  1. Pick one sheet. Just one. The Room-by-Room walk-through is the usual favorite.
  2. Pick one room. The smallest one, or the one that bugs you most.
  3. Set a timer — even 15 minutes counts. The One-Hour Zone Timer sheet shows you how to spend a whole hour without wearing yourself out.
  4. Bag up what leaves the house and log it on the Drop Log. Watching the log fill up is quietly wonderful.
  5. Stop when the timer stops. Ending on purpose is what makes it easy to come back tomorrow.

Printing notes

  • All sheets print on standard US Letter (8.5×11 in). On A4, choose “Fit to page” — everything fits.
  • Print single-sided so you can lay sheets flat while working, or pin one to the fridge.
  • The decision card works beautifully trimmed and taped inside a cupboard door.
  • Every sheet is designed to survive a highlighter, a spilled coffee, and a magnet.

One small promise

None of these pages will ask you to buy anything, sign up for anything, or feel bad about anything. They're just worksheets — warm ones. If a room takes three tries instead of one, you're not behind, you're just thorough.
