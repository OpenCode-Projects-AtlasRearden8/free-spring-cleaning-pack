Freecycle & Donate Drop Log

Proof that the bags really left the house. Morale fuel.

Why log it?

Out-of-sight bags have a sneaky habit of becoming part of the car. One line per bag closes the loop: where it went, when, and whether you'll go back. Watching this page fill up is half the fun of decluttering — it is a receipt for space you bought back.

Method cheat sheet

  • Donate bins/shops — fastest; check accepted lists firstTip: some shops take textiles even when stained.
  • Charity pickups — book one week ahead and let the date force the deadline
  • Freecycle / Buy Nothing — post with a photo, porch pickup, 24 h reply windowGreat for odd items (leg clamp? fish tank stand?).
  • Direct handoff — the friend/neighbor who'd genuinely like it
  • Specialty homes — books to little libraries, towels to animal shelters, eyeglasses to collection boxes
  • Recycle last — electronics, batteries, paint have drop-off points; they're the last resort, not the first bin

Bag go-line checklist

  • Bag is labeled — contents in marker on tape
  • Nothing you re-looked at is in there
  • No one saw it and wants it first
  • Destination decided, not “the car”
  • Drop date on the calendar
  • Log line ready to fill in after drop

Bag rule

A bag that waits more than 7 days gets dropped at the fastest destination, not the best one. Momentum beats optimization.
